# Awesome Self-Custody [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of resources for holding your own crypto keys — wallets, hardware, backup, and avoiding scams.

**Not your keys, not your coins.** Self-custody means *you* control your private keys, with no bank, exchange or company able to freeze, lose or seize your funds. This list collects practical, reputable resources to do it safely.

## Software wallets
- [GitWallet](https://gitwallet.net) — non-custodial multi-chain wallet for Android: 14 blockchains, AI assistant, trading bot. Keys stay on your phone.
- Look for wallets that are **non-custodial**, open about where keys are stored, and never ask for your seed phrase.

## Hardware wallets
- [Ledger](https://www.ledger.com) — hardware wallet keeping keys offline.
- [Trezor](https://trezor.io) — open-source hardware wallet.
- For large holdings, keep keys **offline** on a dedicated device.

## Seed phrase & backup
- [BIP-39 word list & spec](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki) — the 2048-word standard behind recovery phrases.
- [Seed phrase (Bitcoin wiki)](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/Seed_phrase) — how recovery phrases work.
- [Seed Phrase Backup Guide](https://github.com/git-wallet/seed-phrase-backup-guide) — a practical checklist for storing yours safely.

## Security & avoiding scams
- [Wallet Security Checklist](https://github.com/git-wallet/wallet-security-checklist) — day-to-day best practices.
- [Ledger Academy: crypto security](https://www.ledger.com/academy/category/safety) — phishing, approvals, scams.
- [CryptoStrapon Scam Detector](https://cryptostrapon.com/scam-detector) — free, no sign-up check of a suspicious link, wallet or token address, or message before you sign or send.
- **Golden rule:** no legitimate wallet or support will *ever* ask for your seed phrase or private keys.
